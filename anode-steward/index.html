The problem

Owning governance rights is easy. Exercising them institutionally is not.

Governance creates a stream of proposals, deadlines, trade-offs and internal decisions. Most organizations solve parts of the workflow with research, delegation or custody. Steward connects the full decision process.

01 / SIGNAL

Too much governance noise

Dozens of protocols can create hundreds of proposals. Material decisions get buried inside routine governance activity.

02 / DECISION

No consistent institutional logic

Votes are often handled proposal by proposal, without a reusable policy connecting the decision to the institution's mandate.

03 / RECORD

Weak accountability trail

Who reviewed it? Why did we vote? Was it aligned with policy? Steward turns each decision into a structured, auditable record.
